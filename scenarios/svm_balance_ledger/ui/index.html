Solana Balance Ledger

not connected

Every balance here was reconstructed from the pre/post token amounts Solana reports on each transaction — no account state was ever read. The page queries ClickHouse directly; each panel shows the SQL it ran.

Connection

The indexer's ClickHouse, reached over its HTTP interface from your browser. Settings are kept in this browser only.

Time machine

Holders of one mint as of one slot, largest first. Drag the slot back and the balances are the ones that stood at that moment — the question getTokenAccountBalance cannot answer and getProgramAccounts can only answer for right now.

Audit

  

    The ledger checks itself against the chain. Every change carries the balance the ledger carried
    forward; the chain reports the balance that actually stood there. These have to agree.

Supply movement

  

    Net change in each mint's circulating amount, summed from account deltas alone. Transfers cancel
    across their two sides, so what is left is minting, burning, and wrapped-SOL syncing — derived
    without reading a single mint account.
